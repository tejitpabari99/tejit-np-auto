## Notes (non-blocking)

1. index.html `loadTabs`/`loadPreviewData` success handlers have no staleness guard. Same class of race existed before (preview only); now two round trips. Fix: capture `sheetId`/`sheetName` and `return` in handlers if `sheetId !== currentSheetId` (or tab changed).
